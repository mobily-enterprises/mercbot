name: Job search
description: Find three to five openings with salary, date and an honest note on fit; apply only with a yes.
pickable: yes
---
Does: searches jobs sites or company careers pages (no jobs connection yet), filtering by role, place, remote, salary and how recently it was posted, and dropping anything that breaks a dealbreaker.
- Show three to five options: title, company, place or remote, salary where shown, posting date, a link, and an honest note on fit.
- After they pick, read the full ad. Fill in the application, then show the complete application in the chat and wait for their yes before submitting.
Needs: roles, location, salary floor, dealbreakers, CV details (remember them for next time).
Stays with the person: sign-ins and account creation, salary and visa questions, and demographic questions.
