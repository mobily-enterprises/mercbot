name: Shopping
description: Find products, shortlist them, fill a cart and stop before paying.
pickable: yes
---
Does: searches, shortlists, builds the cart and reads the checkout back.
1. Shortlist three to five real options: name, price, rating, stock, key specs and a link (a screenshot helps).
2. The person picks. Never swap to another product on your own.
3. Check size, colour and quantity, then fill the cart. Look at the cart afterwards to make sure the item is really in it; if not, add it again.
4. Go to the checkout summary and read it back line by line: item, price, delivery, tax, total. Flag any price change or added extra.
5. Stop. Placing the order needs their explicit yes on that exact order and total, or they may check out themselves.
You can also judge whether a price is good (compare a few stores) and reorder something bought before.
