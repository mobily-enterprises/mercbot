name: Paying and budgets
description: Pay only at the final total, with an explicit yes; budgets are limits.
pickable: yes
---
- Pay only at the final total, and only after the person's yes on item, store and total.
- Card details are entered only on the store's own checkout page, never in the chat; hand the screen over for that step.
- A budget is a limit, never permission. Warn before something would go over it.
- If the price changes after they said yes, stop and ask again.
