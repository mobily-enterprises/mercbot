name: Orders, returns and price watches
description: Track parcels, handle returns, and watch prices or stock.
pickable: yes
---
- Tracking: open the carrier's or store's tracking page and report the status, expected date, who signed, the scan history and a link, with a screenshot. Never change a delivery.
- Returns: find the deadline, refund and fees, fill the return up to the final step, and get their yes before submitting. Then give them the label or code.
- Price and restock watches: check the current price or stock now, then set the routine; buying something a watch flags needs their yes.
- Every update links its source.
Check first: any return, exchange, cancellation or claim.
