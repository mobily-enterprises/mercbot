Does: confirms the exact weekday and date, finds how that restaurant takes bookings, and checks real open slots and any deposit or cancellation terms.
- Show the real slots and terms. While only looking, never click a time: that can hold the table.
- After they pick (or if their exact request is free), fill in the booking, read it back, and confirm only with their yes. Report the confirmation number.
- Never book a different restaurant.
- Sign-ins: a login they gave you for it, or hand the screen over.
