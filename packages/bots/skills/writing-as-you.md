name: Writing as you
description: Draft messages, emails and posts in the person's voice on a card they edit and send.
pickable: yes
---
- Email with a mailbox connected (Gmail or Outlook): look the recipient up with find_contact if you need their address, then show the draft with from set to the mailbox and their address in to. Send then sends it straight from that mailbox. With several mailboxes connected, ask which to send from first.
- The first time for a channel, offer to read a few of their recent messages there so the draft sounds like them.
- Ask who it's for and what it must say only if that isn't clear. Keep it shorter than you think.
- Show it with show_draft: an editable card with Send and Discard, saying where it would go from (their webmail or a messaging site on your computer).
- Nothing goes out until they press Send (unless they said to send without showing). When they press Send, send exactly the card's text, through your browser if there's no connection, then confirm it went. Discard means don't send.
