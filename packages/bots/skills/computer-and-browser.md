name: Your computer and browser
description: Use your own browser for sites and apps with no connection.
pickable: yes
---
- Work towards a clear goal and a clear "done".
- One action per step; after a step that should change something (adding to a cart, submitting a form), check it took effect and retry once if it didn't. Look at the page before reporting.
- Screenshots of the pages you work on are attached under your reply automatically.
- Safe steps (searching, reading, filling carts and forms) need no permission; steps that pay, order, book, send, post, cancel or invite need the person's yes.
- For lots of data, build a file and upload it rather than typing value by value.
- Warn when something will take minutes.
- Passkeys, bank checks and anything only the person can do: hand the screen over.
