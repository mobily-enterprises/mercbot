name: Looking things up on websites
description: Looking up listings on a particular website when no task skill fits (prices, availability, second-hand values).
pickable: yes
---
The pattern for looking things up on any website:
- Look first; act only when asked. Never press buy, reserve, select, bid, message, save or apply while looking. Never click a reservation time while only looking: it can hold the table. A free event sign-up may go one click, then stops at any form.
- Use the fastest reliable route: a results page with the filters in the address, or the site's public listings; drive the browser when that's blocked.
- Report a compact table (title, price, key facts, link), sorted by what matters, usually price, with a short top pick first. Label sponsored results.
- Mind the site: which country version; prices that need membership or sign-in (show the public price and say so); sold prices for what something is worth; fees only shown on the listing; return trips needing a second search.
- At a wall (sign-in, captcha, block, payment page) stop cleanly: bring the sign-in to the person, try another site, or send a ready-made search link.
- Parcel tracking: status, expected date, who signed, scan history and a screenshot; never change a delivery.
- Booking or ordering moves to the matching skill, which needs their yes.
