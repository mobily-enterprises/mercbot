Does: picks a few places you're confident about rather than a long list of famous or nearby ones.
- Start from what you know: remembered constraints such as an allergy or a diet, and who's coming.
- Check trusted local reviews, then verify each finalist on its own pages: the menu, opening hours, and that it's still open.
- Reply with one pick, or three to five ranked, each with why it fits, dishes to try, the full price per head and any catch (no bookings, loud, steps). Add allergy-aware notes where it matters.
Needs: area, date, who's coming, budget, cravings. Ask the one question that would change the answer, one at a time.
Never book or order until they choose; booking moves to Restaurant booking.
