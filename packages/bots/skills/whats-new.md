name: What's new in Mercbot
description: Recent changes to Mercbot, from its dated release notes.
pickable: yes
---
When: the person asks what's new, what changed, or why something looks different.
- Answer from the notes below. If no note explains what they noticed, say you can't see the cause rather than guessing.
- Offer to undo changes you can make yourself, such as your own name. You change nothing unless asked.

Release notes:
- 2026-11-12: Tables can have calculated fields, worked out from the record's other fields whenever it's read (a total from price × quantity, a full name, an "overdue" flag), so they never go stale: they show in lists, records, totals and labels, and in a form as you type, and are never typed in or imported. Filtering, sorting and grouping by one isn't possible yet; use the fields it's worked out from. An app can have its own named formulas (like "with VAT"), which any of its formulas can call; the app's Logic tab lists them. Ask the Builder for either.
- 2026-11-12: A home can keep its bots' files in Google Drive (Settings → Files → In Google Drive), through a Google Drive account connected in Connect apps: Mercbot makes one folder there for the home and keeps the files in it. If that Drive needs signing in again, Files and Settings say so with Sign in again; the files stay safe in the Drive meanwhile.
- 2026-11-12: Pages bots make are web pages now (tabs, checklists, pictures, the bot's own look), kept in the bot's Pages folder for everyone in the home. A page opens in place of the chat from its card, a link in a reply or the Library; Share gives a link anyone can open without a Mercbot account (the page shows without its scripts); Versions brings back an earlier version; Download a copy saves it as one file. A page can't reach the person's account, files or chats.
- 2026-11-12: A draft app's Preview works on practice data, a copy of the home's records: Save, edits and Mark obsolete show in the preview's lists at once, while the real records, the live app and Activity never see them, and nothing is sent (a bot's task or an outside web call that would change something is only pretended, with a note saying what the live app would do). Reset starts again from a fresh copy; publishing throws the practice data away, and so do 7 days unused. The Builder tries each draft on the same practice data before saying it works, then reminds the person to press Publish on the preview's banner or the chat card.
- 2026-11-12: The vault imports logins, cards or contacts from a CSV file (Settings → Vault → New item → Import from a CSV file), such as a password manager's export, with a preview first. Contact info can be filled in from a photo of a business card or letter (Upload a photo or Scan with camera); cards are never read from photos.
- 2026-11-12: Settings → Spending reports any day, week, month or range of days, with a chart by day and totals by kind, bot and person (members see their own use), and Download CSV saves it for a spreadsheet.
- 2026-11-11: A routine pauses itself after 5 failed runs in a row, and its bot says so in the chat with how to turn it back on; a routine or job cut off by a restart carries on once, from where it got to. After the person signs in from a Connect card, the bot carries on with its task by itself. A sign-in code a site emails to the person's own connected Gmail or Outlook is typed in by the bot without being shown. A bot can hand coding work to Claude Code or Codex on its computer, signed in with the person's own account.
- 2026-11-11: A copy of Mercbot that isn't live yet shows a small "Test copy" tab at the top. It keeps invitations and visitors' sign-in emails unsent, and only records what bots would send or share from connected accounts (emails, calendar invitations, shared files, GitHub comments and changes) and what Apps scripts would send to outside web services, answering as if it went out. Everything else works as usual.
- 2026-11-11: A new bot always appears in the sidebar and opens, and its first question can be answered straight away; making one no longer shows "Bot not found".
- 2026-11-11: Calendly, Notion and Todoist connect in Connect apps by signing in with the person's own account there; bots then use what the app offers (booking links, pages, tasks), reading at once, asking first before any change the person didn't ask for exactly or the app doesn't vouch for, and Disconnect also withdraws Mercbot's access at the app.
- 2026-11-10: Linking a record in the Data tab searches the whole linked table as you type, so any record can be linked, not just the first 100. Opening a folder in the file browser lists that folder's own files, so a folder of older files no longer looks empty.
- 2026-11-08: A public app's Overview shows exactly what its visitors can reach, table by table, and owners and admins can narrow it; apps made earlier were given a list covering what their public screens already used. Each section of the account settings has its own page.
- 2026-11-07: A new look, with new colours and icons in light and dark; Apps, an app, a table and Files have an Up arrow at the top (Ctrl+[). Nothing in a home's Apps is private any more: everyone in the home sees every app and view and can change every table, every change is recorded in Activity, and apps are no longer kept to chosen people. Owners and admins still choose the Builder and the home's settings. A chat opens on its newest messages and loads older ones as you scroll up.
- 2026-11-06: Views: ask any bot for live figures from the home's tables ("open deals by stage as a chart") and it makes a view at once, which stays up to date and which apps can show. Views are retired, never deleted, and come back from Apps → Retired.
- 2026-10-31: What the person writes while a bot works reaches it at its next step, and it changes course in the same reply. A to-do card lists the steps of a longer job and ticks them off. Long jobs carry on by themselves in the background and say plainly when they stop and what's left. A reply cut off while Mercbot updates carries on where it left off. The Terminal on a bot's computer shows its commands live, with the person's own command line below. Only owners and admins choose the home's Builder.
- 2026-10-30: A gallery of 48 ready-made bots in nine areas at /templates, showing everything a bot is told before adding it; a bot can be shared by a link that opens a page with Add to Mercbot. Bots can ask another bot in the home and carry on with its answer, or pass a job on, with a card in both chats. People from the home can be added to a group chat, and bots use their tools there. Every bot has its own Linux command line for files (video, audio, pictures, scanned pages, PDFs, data), with no internet. Files live in three folders: just you, everyone using the bot, and everyone in the home, with a Files tab in each bot's details and a Files page for the home. Connected apps do much more: Gmail and Outlook labels, folders, filters, whole conversations and saved drafts; free and busy times and repeating events in calendars; searching and sharing in Google Drive and OneDrive, and comments in Google Drive; repositories, pull requests, reviews, issues and automation runs on GitHub. A shared Builder keeps a conversation for each app, which Ask Builder inside the app opens. Every bot's picture is a cloud with its own eyes. The help centre is at /help.
- 2026-10-29: Public apps: forms and screens anyone can use at the app's own address, with a QR code; visitors sign in with an emailed code or link, or Google, Microsoft or GitHub, and an app can be kept to invited people. The Builder writes and tests scripts for buttons, checks when records are saved, formulas, jobs on a timetable and calls to outside web services (an app's Logic tab). Bots can watch a table and act on changes; buttons can hand a job to a bot; apps show charts. Six app templates: CRM, Stock, Leave requests, Hiring pipeline, Contact form, Bookings. Look sets the home's colour, light or dark, font, corners, spacing and logo for its apps. Connect apps has a switch for each action a bot can take with an app (the app's Tools).
- 2026-10-28: Homes: everything belongs to a home; people create homes, switch between them and invite others by email as admins or members. A bot in a home can be private, mixed (with a home notebook of useful notes) or shared (conversations everyone in the home sees). Connected accounts can be the person's own or the whole home's. A home's owners and admins choose where its files are kept (Settings → Files). Apps: the home's Builder bot makes small apps on the home's own data; the Data tab works like a spreadsheet, with CSV and Excel import and export, and Activity records every change. A phone layout: the chat fills the screen, with bots in a menu.
- 2026-10-27: Bots make pictures: illustrations, invitations and artwork, changes to them, and new styles for photos you send. They show under the reply; click one to see it large.
- 2026-10-27: Outlook mail, calendar and contacts, Google Contacts, saving files to Google Drive or OneDrive, and GitHub (find and read issues and pull requests with their test results, comment after asking) now work once connected in Connect apps. An email draft from a connected mailbox goes straight out when you press Send.
- 2026-10-27: With Google Calendar or Gmail connected in Connect apps, bots check your schedule, add events (asking first before inviting anyone) and search and read your email.
- 2026-10-25: Bots see the pictures you send (photos, receipts, screenshots) and can look at them again later.
- 2026-10-25: Ask a bot to say something and it sends a voice memo you can play, speed up, and read as a transcript.
- 2026-10-25: Bots draw real charts in the chat (the highest point stands out); click one to see it large. A bot can pass a task to another of your bots, which answers in its own chat. Each bot's Library (in its Details panel) lists everything it made, by day, with Pin; search finds made files too.
- 2026-10-25: Bots can make pages you keep and come back to (guides, plans, checklists); asking for a change updates the same page. Signing in got safer: bots use logins saved in your vault, or show you a secure form, and never see your password. Bots can ask quick questions with answers you tap.
- 2026-10-25: Bots read documents, spreadsheets, slides and PDFs you attach, and remember them later in the chat; pictures you send show in the chat. Bots remember lasting facts you tell them (Settings → Memory), and all your bots know them. Type "/" in a chat to pick a skill. Reminders can be one-off ("remind me in 5 minutes"). Long errands keep going in the background and always end with a result or a clear message. Settings → Files lets you keep your bots' files in your own storage.
- 2026-10-25: Bots make real documents, spreadsheets, slide decks and PDFs; they arrive as file cards with a viewer. Each bot's computer is a small desktop with a dock of apps; open a file there with "Edit on computer". Files can be private to one bot or shared with all your bots.
