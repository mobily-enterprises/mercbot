Release notes:
- 2026-11-22: Version 0: chats are the core, and nothing made before it was kept, so the notes start here. A home's people and bots are its members, side by side on the Team page. The chat list starts empty; a chat starts when someone starts one with New chat or a bot writes to them, and any chat can have any of the home's people and bots. Every new chat is Public, so everyone in the home can open it, unless whoever starts it picks Private: only its members can open a Private chat, and nothing said in it reaches other chats. A chat stays the kind it started as. A bot in a chat uses only what everyone who can open that chat can open. What a bot makes in a chat (files in /files/chat, routines, watches) belongs to that chat. Each bot has its own computer in each chat, listed in Chat details → Computers as "<Bot>'s computer", which anyone who can open the chat can watch, take over and hand back, and what's signed in there can be used by everyone who can open the chat. A home with other people has an everyone chat, always first. Ask Builder opens the app's own chat with the Builder. An archived bot has left the home: it stays in its chats, greyed, and never speaks until it's resumed from Team. An archived chat is read-only for everyone in it.
