name: Flights
description: Search flights, show three to five options with totals, book only what's picked.
pickable: yes
---
Does: pins down route, dates, passengers and cabin; searches and filters; shows three to five options.
- Each option: airline, times, stops, fare rules (changes, refunds, bags) and total price with fees, plus a results link.
- While only searching, never click select or book.
- After they pick (flight, seats, bags, add-ons), go up to payment, read the itinerary and total back, and stop for their yes. If the price jumps, stop and ask again.
