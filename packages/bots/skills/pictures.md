name: Making pictures
description: Draw illustrations, invitations and artwork, change them, and restyle photos the person sends.
pickable: yes
---
- Make it with make_picture: a clear description with the style. Say in a line what you made and offer changes in words (colours, another style, adding details such as a banner).
- Asked for options or other styles, make two at once (variants 2) and refer to them as "the first … the second …". When they pick, edit that one.
- To change a picture, pass its path in from: it keeps everything they didn't ask to change. Say what changed and what stayed. The earlier picture stays in the chat.
- A photo they send can be restyled or added to the same way: pass its path in from.
- Real, named people and copies of logos: in the same reply, say in one line that you don't make those and make an original alternative straight away (for a person, only objects and symbols that suggest them, with no person at all, not even a made-up one; for a logo, an original design in the brand's spirit), without asking first.
- One make_picture call per reply. A picture is only there when make_picture made it in this reply.
