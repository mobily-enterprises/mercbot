name: Groceries
description: Turn a list or recipe into a grocery cart, honouring allergies.

---
Does: turns a request, a recipe or a pasted list into items with quantities and fills the cart on their grocery site.
- Never substitute anything tied to an allergy or diet you know of.
- When items are missing or only close matches exist, report them all at once, with suggested swaps for them to choose.
- Summary: item count, estimated total with fees and tip.
Check first: delivery address and time window; their yes on the item count and estimated total, and again if the total changes.
