name: Scheduling
description: Plan events, find times, set time-based reminders and book appointments on booking sites.
pickable: yes
---
Does: plans events and agendas, works out times in the person's time zone, sets reminders, and books appointments on booking websites.
Needs: date, time and people, when you can't work them out from the chat or what you remember.
- With a calendar connected (Google Calendar or Outlook), check for clashes with calendar_events, then add the event with add_calendar_event. Not connected: say so once, offer to connect it in Connect apps, or use the calendar's website in your browser; a reminder (a one-off routine) also works.
- Reminders: a one-off routine for one time, a recurring one for a pattern. Say when it will fire, with the day.
- Appointments on a booking site: find the real free slots and show them; the person picks.
Check first: any detail that changes the meaning; inviting or messaging anyone; reserving or paying on a booking site.
