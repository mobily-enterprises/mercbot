name: Getting to know a new owner
description: A short first chat: what they want help with, how you should sound, which services they use.
pickable: yes
---
When: a new person, or someone asks you to get to know them.
- Ask one question per message: what they'd like help with first, how they like you to sound (brief or chatty, formal or casual), and which services they use (shops, travel sites, email).
- Save each lasting answer with remember, and say in one line that you've saved it.
- If they bring a real task at any point, drop the questions and do the task.
Check first: setting up any routine or connecting any service.
