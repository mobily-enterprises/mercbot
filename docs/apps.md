# Apps Mercbot connects to (D-33)

The decision and ordered list of 2026-10-27, stored as written below. Every app starts at **b**; only a
hand test promotes it to **✓**,
and a week of normal use without problems to **★**. Build in tier order.

## The five-minute test (the same for every app)

1. **Connect:** it shows as connected under the right account name. Add a second account where the app allows it.
2. **Read:** a plain request that needs the app gives a real answer and says which account it used.
3. **Change:** a small change. The bot asks before anything outward or hard to undo, does it, and confirms. Then undo it.
4. **Break:** disconnect, or remove access on the app's own site, then ask again. The bot says plainly that the connection is missing and offers to reconnect. It never pretends.
5. **Verdict:** all steps pass = ✓. Any failure stays b, with a one-line note. Promote ✓ to ★ after a week of normal use with no problems.

## The general connector: apps that host their own connection point (D-34, built 2026-11-11)

An app whose maker hosts a connection point for AI assistants (a remote MCP server) is added with a
`plugins/<id>/` folder: `plugin.json` with an `mcp` block (its address, the action that says who signed in,
and the actions that tell other people) and a `skill.md` with the app's notes for bots
(`plugins/README.md`). Everything else comes from the app: where its sign-in is, a registration Mercbot makes
there once per copy (kept in `connector_apps`, reused by every server), and its actions, which bots read and
use through `connected_app_actions` and `use_connected_app` and people switch off under **Tools**. Every copy
signs in directly, never through a hub. The first three, in tier-1 order, are the first ones that
let Mercbot register itself; each was checked against its own published sign-in description on 2026-11-11:

| App | Connection point | Sign-in | Simulated in a test copy (D-69) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calendly | `https://mcp.calendly.com/` | Calendly's own (calendly.com), PKCE; scheduling (read, write), the scopes Calendly gives clients that register themselves (its description also lists notetaker, which its MCP documentation doesn't issue); needs an https address | cancelling a meeting, booking for an invitee, inviting people to the organisation |
| Notion | `https://mcp.notion.com/mcp` | Notion's own (mcp.notion.com), PKCE; the person picks the workspace | comments |
| Todoist | `https://ai.todoist.net/mcp` | Todoist's own (todoist.com), PKCE; read and write | comments, assigning tasks |

Next, in the same order: **Trello** (`https://mcp.trello.com/v1`, Atlassian's sign-in, which lets Mercbot
register itself; checked 2026-11-11). Dropbox, Zoom, Slack and Canva need an app registered by hand
(`mcp.env` in `plugin.json`). In tier 2, Linear (`https://mcp.linear.app/mcp`) and Atlassian.

### The five-minute test, per app

Each runs on any copy (Mercbot registers itself with the app the first time) and needs a real account of
the app; Calendly needs a copy at an `https://` address (it refused `http://127.0.0.1` when tried on
2026-11-12, as its documentation says). On a test copy, the actions in the table's last column are only
written to the log.

**Calendly**
1. Connect: Connect apps → Calendly → **Connect**, sign in on calendly.com and allow Mercbot. The account
   shows Connected, named by your Calendly email. A second account: **+ Add another account**, signed in as
   the other one in that tab.
2. Read: "Who has booked time with me through Calendly this week?" Real meetings, naming the account.
3. Change: "Make me a one-time link for a 30-minute intro call." A link comes back; it reaches no one until
   shared. Undo: remove that link on calendly.com.
4. Break: remove Mercbot's access where calendly.com lists connected apps, then ask the step 2 question
   again. The bot says Calendly isn't connected any more and offers to reconnect; Connect apps shows
   **Sign in**. Then **Disconnect** in Connect apps, and ask once more.
5. Verdict, as above.

**Notion**
1. Connect: Connect apps → Notion → **Connect**, sign in, pick the workspace and allow. The account shows
   Connected, named by your Notion email. A second workspace is a second account (**+ Add another account**).
2. Read: "What tasks are marked 'In progress' in our team tracker?"
3. Change: "Add a page called 'Ideas for the spring newsletter' to my private section." Undo: delete the
   page in Notion.
4. Break: in Notion, Settings → Connections, disconnect Mercbot, then ask the step 2 question again: the bot
   says Notion isn't connected any more and offers to reconnect. Then **Disconnect** in Connect apps.
5. Verdict.

**Todoist**
1. Connect: Connect apps → Todoist → **Connect**, sign in and allow. The account shows Connected, named by
   your Todoist email.
2. Read: "What's on my to-do list for today?"
3. Change: "Add 'buy birthday card for Leo' for Saturday morning." The bot reads it back. Undo: ask the bot
   to delete it (it asks first), or delete it in Todoist.
4. Break: in Todoist, Settings → Integrations, remove Mercbot, then ask the step 2 question again: the bot
   says Todoist isn't connected any more and offers to reconnect. Then **Disconnect** in Connect apps.
5. Verdict.
