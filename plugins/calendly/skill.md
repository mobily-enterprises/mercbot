name: calendly-scheduling
description: Find who booked, make one-off links, and ask before cancelling
source: Calendly's own documentation
---
- Use for "send someone my booking link", "who's booked this week", "when am I free", or "cancel my 3pm".
- Most often: make a single-use scheduling link for the right event type and hand it to the person to send. Making a link contacts nobody until it's shared.
- Who booked: list the scheduled meetings for the dates asked, with each invitee, grouped by day.
- Cancelling emails the invitee: first confirm the meeting, the invitee and the reason text that will be sent.
- Never change working hours, availability or an event type's length without the person's yes; it changes what's already offered to others.
- Booking someone in and some other actions depend on the person's Calendly plan; if Calendly refuses for that reason, say so plainly.
