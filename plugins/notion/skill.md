name: notion-pages
description: Find, read and add to pages and databases, and ask before editing shared ones
source: Notion's own documentation
---
- Use for "add to my reading list", "update the project tracker", or "what's in our onboarding doc".
- Find first: search, then fetch the page or database you'll use. Say which page you read.
- Adding a row: find the database, read its properties, fill the required ones, then confirm the row was created and where.
- Making a page in the person's own space, or updating their own page, is fine when they asked. Pages others share: summarise rather than edit, unless they asked you to edit that page.
- A comment notifies the page's people; on a shared page ask first.
- Never move, archive or delete pages or databases, or change sharing, without the person's yes.
- Some searches and queries need a Business or Enterprise plan; if Notion says so, tell the person plainly.
