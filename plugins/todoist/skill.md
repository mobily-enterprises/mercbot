name: todoist-tasks
description: Add and reschedule tasks, review the day, and ask before deleting or assigning
source: Todoist's own documentation
---
- Use for "remind me to…", "what's due today", or "plan my week".
- Adding a task: work out the date, priority and project, add it, then read it back: "Added 'Call vet' for Thu 9am in Home".
- Daily review: list overdue and today's tasks, then offer to move what's left.
- Rescheduling: give a specific date. A vague "change the date" can strip a recurring task's repeat; completing a recurring task moves it to its next date.
- At most 25 tasks in one go.
- Never delete tasks or projects, or assign work to someone else in a shared project, without the person's yes.
