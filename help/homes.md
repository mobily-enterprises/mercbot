# Homes: Mercbot for families and teams

Everything in Mercbot lives in a home: its bots, their files and computers, its Apps and the people who use them. A home can be just you, a household sharing a family calendar, or a small team sharing an inbox and a sales pipeline. Everyone in a home works with the same bots, but each person's chats with a bot stay their own unless the home decides otherwise. This page explains what belongs to a home and what stays personal, how bots and accounts are shared, Apps in a home, spending, and a setup checklist to start from.

## What a home holds

Mercbot makes your first home, called "*your first name*'s home", with you as its owner, and you can create more. Each home is separate: its bots, files and data aren't visible from your other homes.

| Belongs to the home | Stays personal to you |
|---|---|
| Its bots and their routines | Your chats with each bot |
| The shared files folder | The files a bot keeps for you in its own folder |
| Accounts added for **Everyone in** the home | Accounts you add as **Just me** |
| A mixed bot's home notebook | What your bots remember about you |
| Apps, their tables and records | Your vault of logins, cards and contacts |
| Where the home keeps its files | Your **Auto-review** switch and your rules |
| The home's spending | Your group chats |

Owners and admins can't read your private chats; they choose how each bot is shared.

## Create and switch homes

The current home's name sits at the top of the sidebar, with a small coloured tile showing its initial.

To switch homes:

1. Click the home's name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose a home from the list. Each one shows your role there, and a tick marks the one you're in.

To create a home:

1. Click the home's name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Create a home**.
3. Type a name (for example, "The Smiths" or "Shop team") and press **Create**.

You're the owner of every home you create. The sidebar, bots, Connect apps and Apps all change to the home you're in. Each home keeps its own pinned bots, sections and pinned apps for you.

## Bots in a home

Every bot belongs to a home, and everyone in the home can use it. Anyone can add a bot with **New bot**. Deleting a bot removes it, with its conversations, for everyone, so agree on it first.

### Who sees what

Owners and admins choose how each bot is shared, in the bot's **Details** panel under **Who sees what**. Everyone else can see the choice.

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Private** | Each person's chats with the bot are their own. The bot keeps nothing across people |
| **Mixed** (the default) | Chats are private. What helps everyone goes in the home's notebook, which everyone can read |
| **Shared** | Everyone in the home talks with the bot in named conversations they all see |

Group chats are always your own, even when their bots belong to the home. A group chat can't be shared.

### Shared conversations

A shared bot shows its conversations as tabs under the bot, starting with one called "General".

- **New conversation** adds a tab. It starts from what the open conversation knows at that moment; later messages in the original don't flow into it. Every tab needs a name.
- Double-click a tab to rename it.
- Every message shows who wrote it, and the bot reads each message with the writer's name.
- Unread markers are kept for each person.
- In shared conversations the bot never uses anyone's personal memories, connected accounts, saved logins or own skills. It uses only the home's accounts.

### The home notebook

A mixed bot keeps a notebook for the home. When a chat teaches it something that helps everyone (how the family likes its shopping list, where the team keeps invoices), it notes it and tells you in one line, such as "Noted for The Smiths: …", with **Undo**.

- It never notes anything personal. It refuses email addresses, long numbers and your saved passwords.
- The bot reads the notebook as information, never other people's chats.
- Everyone can read the notebook in the bot's Details panel.
- A note's author, owners and admins can remove it.
- Turn on **Keep my chats with** *bot* **out of it** to keep your chats out of the notebook entirely.

## Your accounts and the home's

Connected accounts can belong to you or to the whole home.

- **Just me** (the default when connecting): your own account, such as your personal Gmail. Bots use it only in your own chats. Nobody else's chats can reach it.
- **Everyone in** *home*: an account for everyone, such as a family calendar or a team inbox. Only owners and admins can add, sign in, rename or remove one. Everyone sees it listed in **Connect apps**, marked "Everyone in *home* · added by *name*"; members see its status only.

In your own chats, a bot can use your accounts and the home's. In a shared bot's conversations it uses only the home's, because everyone can see them. When several accounts fit a request (your Gmail and the family Gmail), the bot asks which one and keeps to your answer in that chat.

Owners and admins who haven't added an app for themselves still see **+ Add an account for everyone in** *home*. Removing a home's account asks "Remove it for everyone?" first.

Switching actions on and off in an app's **Tools** is personal: each person chooses for their own bots.

## Files, computers and memory

**Files.** Each bot has two folders: one it keeps with you, and the home's shared folder, which everyone in the home and all its bots can see. Bots save in their own folder unless you ask them to share. In a shared bot's conversations, the bot keeps its files in one folder that everyone using it shares.

**Where files are kept.** Owners and admins choose in **Settings**, under **Files**: **On Mercbot**, **In Google Drive** (a Google Drive account connected in Connect apps, where Mercbot makes one folder for the home), or **In the home's own storage** (Nextcloud or any storage with a WebDAV address, with a username and password). Mercbot checks the storage before saving the choice and never shows the password again. Changing it moves all the home's files to the new place, which becomes an exact copy of them; meanwhile they can be read but not changed.

**Computers.** With a private or mixed bot, each person gets their own browser on the bot's computer, so one person's website sign-ins never appear for anyone else. A shared bot's conversation has one browser that everyone in it uses. **Settings**, **Computer**, **Reset** restarts your own computers in the home; shared conversations' computers stay as they are.

**Memory.** What your bots remember about you (an allergy, a preference) is yours and is shared only between your bots in that home. You can see and remove it in **Settings**, under **Memory**.

**Vault.** Your saved logins, cards and contacts are personal and never used in shared conversations.

## Apps in a home

Each home has its own Apps: small apps its bots build on the home's own data, such as a contacts list, a stock tracker or a sales pipeline.

- **One Builder per home.** One bot holds the Builder role and makes and changes apps when people ask. A person previews a draft and publishes it.
- **One conversation per app.** If the Builder is shared, it keeps a separate conversation for each app that everyone can follow; **Ask Builder** inside an app opens it. If it isn't, an owner or admin may be asked once whether to share it.
- **Everyone's bots read and add to the same data** from chat, and any bot can watch a table and act when records change.
- **Pins are personal.** Each person pins up to 8 apps to their sidebar.

Nothing in a home's Apps is private: everyone in the home uses every app and view, and reads and changes every table. Owners and admins choose the home's settings:

| Control | Choices |
|---|---|
| The Builder | Which bot of the home builds its apps |
| How apps look | The home's colour, light or dark, font, corners, spacing and logo |
| Templates | Installed straight from **Templates**, or by asking the Builder |
| Scripts | Switched off and on; which outside addresses they may call |

Every change to the home's records is recorded in **Activity**, with who made it.

## Spending

A home's spending is what its bots' work cost at the AI providers, counted for the home, whoever the work was for. There are no seats or per-person allowances. **Settings** → **Spending** shows:

- **For owners and admins:** the whole home's spending, by day, kind, bot and person.
- **For members:** their own use (what the home's bots did for them).

Each bot shows this month's figure beside its name in the sidebar, the same way: the whole home's for owners and admins, your own for members. Spending is only reported: the home's bots never stop because of it.

## Recommended setup

A checklist for a new family or team home:

1. Create the home and give it a name everyone will recognise.
2. Decide who sees what for each bot: keep mixed for everyday helpers, private for anything personal, and shared for a bot the whole home talks to together.
3. Add shared accounts (the family calendar, the team inbox) as **Everyone in** the home. Ask everyone to connect their own accounts as **Just me**.
4. Choose where the home keeps its files, if you'd rather use your own storage.
5. Pick a Builder for Apps. Everyone in the home sees all of its apps and data, so keep anything some members mustn't read out of them.
6. Ask everyone to check their own **Auto-review** switch and rules in Settings.
7. Look at the notebooks and the **Activity** feed from time to time, and tidy what's no longer right.

## Questions

### Can the owner or admins read my chats?

No. Your chats with a private or mixed bot are yours. Only shared bots' conversations are seen by everyone, and the bot says so.

### Whose spending is a bot's work?

The home's: every bot's work counts in the home's spending, whoever it was for. Owners and admins see the whole home's spending in **Settings** → **Spending**; members see their own use.
