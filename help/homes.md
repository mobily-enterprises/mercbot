# Homes: Mercbot for families and businesses

Everything in Mercbot lives in a home: its people and bots, their chats, files and computers, and its Apps. A home can be just you, a household sharing a family calendar, or a small business sharing an inbox and a sales pipeline. Everyone in a home works with the same bots, in chats that are Public unless someone chooses Private. This page explains what belongs to a home and what stays yours, bots and accounts in a home, Apps, spending, and a setup checklist to start from.

## What a home holds

Mercbot makes your first home, called "*your first name*'s home", with you as its owner, and you can rename it or create more. Each home is separate: its people, bots, chats, files and data aren't visible from your other homes.

| Belongs to the home | Stays yours alone |
|---|---|
| Its people and bots, under **People** and **Bots** in the sidebar | Your Private rooms, shared only with their members, and your private chats with a person or a bot |
| Its Public rooms, which everyone in it can open | Your own skills |
| What each chat's bots make there, with the chat | Your **Auto-review** switch and your rules |
| The shared files folder | Your pins, sections, folded sections and unread marks |
| Every account connected in the home, and the vault | |
| Apps, their tables and records | |
| Where the home keeps its files, and its spending | |

Every new room is Public, so everyone in the home, owners and admins included, can open it. Choose **Private** when you make a room to keep it to its members. Select a person under **People** in the sidebar to open your private chat with just the two of you; your chat with a bot is private too. See [Rooms, people and bots](/help/chats).

## Create and switch homes

The current home's name sits at the top of the sidebar, with a small coloured tile showing its initial.

To switch homes:

1. Click the home's name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose a home from the list. Each one shows your role there, and a tick marks the one you're in.

To create a home:

1. Click the home's name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Create a home**.
3. Under **Name your home**, type its name (for example, "The Smiths").
4. Press **Create home**.

The new home opens with Mercbot's welcome chat, which helps you add the bots you need, or use **+** beside **Bots** in the sidebar (see [Start with a ready-made set](#start-with-a-ready-made-set)).

You're the owner of every home you create. The sidebar's rooms, people and bots, Connect apps and Apps all change to the home you're in. Each home keeps its own pinned chats, sections, folded sections and pinned apps for you.

## Start with a ready-made set

Pick what you need help with, and Mercbot adds a few bots that work well together: the six that help run a busy household, say, or the five a small shop needs. Since each home has its own bots, files and Apps, a set usually goes with a home of its own:

- **A new home's welcome chat:** once you've named Mercbot, while the home has no bot but it, Mercbot asks "What's this home for? I can suggest a few bots to help with it." **Describe what I need** opens **Add bots** on **Describe what you need**, and **Show me ready-made teams** opens it on its ready-made sets; nothing is added until you say so there. **Not now**, or typing a message instead, skips the question.
- **Add bots**, the **+** beside **Bots**, adds a set to the home you're in at any time with **A ready-made set**, for example when a company adds another part of its work. See [A ready-made set](/help/bots#a-ready-made-set).
- When no set fits, **Describe what you need** in **Add bots**, and Mercbot suggests bots for it, including new ones written for your home. See [Describe what you need](/help/bots#describe-what-you-need).

Each bot in a set is one of Mercbot's templates and starts as any new bot does: under **Bots**, with its template's greeting waiting for you when you start a chat with it. Nothing runs until you answer it, so adding a set costs no AI work. A bot whose template the home already has isn't added twice. Anyone in a home can add a set, as anyone can add a bot.

- **Design studio**: Crit (critique), Tooltip (interface words), Ramp (accessibility), Tuning Fork (voice), Lectern (slides)

## Rename a home

A home's owners and admins can change its name, the first home Mercbot made for you included:

1. Open **Settings** → **General**.
2. Under **Home**, type the new name in **Home name**.
3. Select **Save**. **Escape** puts back the name it has.

Everyone in the home sees the new name at once, at the top of the sidebar and in the list of homes. Its bots, people and address stay as they were. A name can't be empty, and has at most 160 characters. Members see the name in **Settings** → **General**, and that owners and admins change it.

## Bots in a home

Every bot is a member of its home, listed under **Bots** in the sidebar, below the home's people, and anyone in the home can start a chat with it or invite it into a room. Anyone can add bots with **Add bots**. Archiving a bot takes it out of the home's bots for everyone, so agree on it first. See [Archive a bot](/help/bots#archive-a-bot).

A bot in a chat uses only what everyone who can open that chat can also open: in any chat, every Public room of the home; from a Private chat, also the Private rooms that all of its members are in; and never a Private room from a Public one. Nothing reaches your private chats with a person or a bot from another chat. What it makes in a chat belongs to that chat. See [Rooms, people and bots](/help/chats).

### The chat for everyone

Mercbot's welcome chat asks a home's owners and admins "Will anyone else use this home?" **Just me** makes nothing. **Me and other people** makes a chat for everyone in the home, named after the home, with the main bot in it. Everyone who joins the home is in it, and it's always the first row under each person's **Rooms**. You leave it only by leaving the home, and nobody can take a person out of it; the person who started it and the home's owners and admins can take a bot out. See [The everyone chat](/help/chats#the-everyone-chat).

## The home's connected accounts

Every account connected in **Connect apps** belongs to the home it was connected in. Everyone in the home, and every bot there, can use it in any chat, Public or Private. Each account's line says so, such as **Everyone in** *home*, **and their bots, can read and send from** *address*, and who added it.

- **Anyone in the home can add an account.** Connect one only if you're happy for everyone in the home to use it. To keep an account to yourself, connect it in a home only you are in.
- **Who changes it:** the person who added it, and the home's owners and admins, rename it, sign it in again, choose its pace and remove it. Removing asks "Remove it for everyone in *home*?" first.
- **Emails still say whose they are.** A draft card shows which mailbox it's sent from, and nothing goes until someone answers it, as with any card.
- **Sign-in codes stay with their owner.** A bot that reads a mailbox someone else connected doesn't see the sign-in codes or sign-in links in it.
- **Someone who leaves takes their accounts.** When a person is removed from the home, the accounts they connected there are removed too, and the **Activity** tab in **Apps** lists each one.

When several accounts fit a request (two Gmail accounts), the bot asks which one and keeps to your answer in that chat. Switching actions on and off in an app's **Tools** applies to the whole home, and only its owners and admins can.

## Files, computers and the vault

**Files.** Each chat has its own folder, where its bots keep what they make there; everyone who can open the chat can open it. Each bot also has a folder of its own, and the home has a shared folder that everyone in the home and all its bots can see. From a Private chat, a bot asks you on a card before putting something in its own folder or the shared one.

**Where files are kept.** Owners and admins choose in **Settings**, under **Files**: **On Mercbot**, **In Google Drive** (a Google Drive account connected in Connect apps, where Mercbot makes one folder for the home), or **In the home's own storage** (Nextcloud or any storage with a WebDAV address, with a username and password). Mercbot checks the storage before saving the choice and never shows the password again. Changing it moves all the home's files to the new place, which becomes an exact copy of them; meanwhile they can be read but not changed.

**Computers.** In each chat, each bot has its own computer, labelled with its name, such as "Chef's computer". It belongs to that chat: everyone who can open the chat can watch it, take it over and use what's signed in there. See [The bots' computers](/help/chats#the-bots-computers).

**Vault.** The home's saved logins, cards and contacts are the home's, like its accounts. See [The vault](/help/vault).

## Apps in a home

Each home has its own Apps: small apps its bots build on the home's own data, such as a contacts list, a stock tracker or a sales pipeline.

- **One Builder per home.** One bot holds the Builder role and makes and changes apps when people ask. A person previews a draft and publishes it.
- **One chat per app.** Each app has its own Public chat with the Builder, named after the app, which everyone can follow: **Ask Builder** inside an app opens it, and makes it the first time anyone asks.
- **Everyone's bots read and add to the same data** from chat, and any bot can watch a table and act when records change.
- **Pins are personal.** Each person pins up to 8 apps to their sidebar.

Nothing in a home's Apps is private: everyone in the home uses every app and view, and reads and changes every table. Owners and admins choose the home's settings:

| Control | Choices |
|---|---|
| The Builder | Which bot of the home builds its apps |
| How apps look | The home's colour, light or dark, font, corners, spacing and logo |
| Templates | Installed straight from **Templates**, or by asking the Builder |
| Scripts | Switched off and on; which outside addresses they may call |

Every change to the home's records is recorded in **Activity**, with who made it.

## Spending

A home's spending is what its bots' work cost at the AI providers, counted for the home, whoever the work was for. There are no seats or per-person allowances. **Settings** → **Spending** shows:

- **For owners and admins:** the whole home's spending, by day, kind, bot and person.
- **For members:** their own use (what the home's bots did for them).

Spending is only reported: the home's bots never stop because of it.

## Recommended setup

A checklist for a new family or team home:

1. Create the home and give it a name everyone will recognise.
2. In the welcome chat, answer **Me and other people**, so the home has a chat for everyone. Then invite the people and add the bots you need, with the **+** beside **People** and **Bots** in the sidebar.
3. Connect the accounts the home will share (the family calendar, the shared inbox). Remind everyone that an account they connect here is used by everyone in the home.
4. Choose where the home keeps its files, if you'd rather use your own storage.
5. Pick a Builder for Apps. Everyone in the home sees all of its apps and data, so keep anything some members mustn't read out of them.
6. Ask everyone to check their own **Auto-review** switch and rules in Settings.
7. Agree that anything only some of you should read goes in a Private room, and look at the **Activity** feed from time to time.

## Questions

### Can the owner or admins read my chats?

They can open Public rooms, as everyone in the home can. A Private room is opened only by its members, and your chat with a bot or with another person only by the two of you. See [Rooms, people and bots](/help/chats).

### Whose spending is a bot's work?

The home's: every bot's work counts in the home's spending, whoever it was for. Owners and admins see the whole home's spending in **Settings** → **Spending**; members see their own use.
