# Release notes

What's new in Mercbot, newest first. To see which version you're using, open the account menu, choose **Help and support**, then **About Mercbot**. You can also ask any bot "What's new in Mercbot?"

## 22 November 2026

Version 0: chats are at the heart of Mercbot. Nothing made before version 0 was kept, so these notes start here.

- **Bots are members of your home,** like its people. **Team**, above your chats with **Files** and **Apps**, lists everyone in the home, people and bots, each with **Message**. Select one to open their card: a bot's has its **Details** (instructions, Knowledge, apps, routines and archiving), **Files**, **Library** and **Computer**.
- **The chat list is your chats,** and it starts empty: a chat appears when you start one or a bot writes to you. **New chat** (**Ctrl+Alt+G**) brings together any people and bots of the home, and lists the Public chats you can join.
- **Public by default, Private when you choose:** every new chat is Public, so everyone in the home can open it. Choose **Private** when you start a chat to keep it to its members: "Only its members can open it, and nothing in it reaches other chats." A chat stays the kind it started as.
- **One rule for what bots use:** a bot in a chat uses only what everyone who can open that chat can also open. What a bot makes in a chat belongs to that chat, and its files go in the chat's own folder.
- **Each bot's computer in a chat** is listed in **Chat details** → **Computers** as, for example, "Chef's computer", says that it belongs to the chat and who can watch it and use what's signed in on it, and lists what's **Signed in on it**. Anyone who can open the chat can **Watch**, **Take over** and **Hand back**, and the chat shows a line for each take-over and hand-back.
- **The everyone chat:** in a home with other people, a chat for everyone in it, always first in each person's list.
- **Ask Builder** opens the app's own chat with the Builder, made the first time anyone asks.
- **Archiving a bot** means it has left the home: it stays in its chats, greyed out, and never answers, and whoever made it, or an owner or admin, can resume it from **Team**.
- **Archiving a Public chat** puts it away, read-only, for everyone in it, under **Archived chats**, and pauses its routines and watches. A Private chat isn't archived: each of its members hides it for themselves.
- **Bots remember** the Public chats they're in, the way a person in your home would, and bring them up in their other chats; every bot also reads the notes people asked a bot to tell everyone. What's said in a Private chat stays in that chat. See [What bots remember](/help/memory).
- **People in chats:** type **@** to mention someone in the chat (their chat list shows **@** until they read it), react to other people's messages, and attach files, which go in the chat's own folder. In your chat with one bot, **Continue with others…** carries it on in a new chat, starting from the bot's short note of it.
- **Bots join in when useful:** in a chat with several people, its maker or an owner or admin can switch this on in **Chat details**. Then, when someone writes without calling a bot, at most one bot answers if it can help. It's off unless someone switches it on.
- **Discuss with the bots:** in a chat with two or more bots, write a question and choose **+** → **Discuss with the bots**. The card says what it may cost; the bots talk it over a round at a time, each adding something new or passing, and you choose **One more round**, **Wrap up** or **Stop**.

See [Chats and your team](/help/chats).
