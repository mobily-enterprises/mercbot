# General questions and answers

Short answers to the questions people ask most about Mercbot, with links to the pages that go into more detail.

## What is Mercbot?

Mercbot gives you bots that do real work for you. You ask in plain words, and a bot looks things up, works in websites, uses the apps you connect, makes documents and spreadsheets, and keeps going while you're away. Results arrive in your chat. See [Welcome to Mercbot](/help/welcome).

## How is a bot different from a chat assistant?

A bot doesn't just answer. It has its own computer in the cloud with a web browser and office apps, it can use your connected email, calendar and files, and it can do things on a schedule. It works with you and your family or team in chats, asks before anything risky, and can hand work to other bots in the home.

## Who is Mercbot for?

Families, small businesses and companies. Everyone gets their own home, and can create more homes, such as one for the family and one for work. See [Example jobs for a bot](/help/example-jobs) for ideas.

## Where do my bots run?

On Mercbot's servers, not on your device. A bot works on a small cloud computer: a desktop with a browser and office apps. It has one in each chat it's in, which belongs to that chat: it's labelled with the bot's name, such as "Ledger's computer", and everyone who can open the chat can watch it and take over. See [The bots' computer and connected apps](/help/computer-and-apps).

## Do bots keep working when I close Mercbot?

Yes. A job you've started carries on in the background, and routines run at their set time whether you're online or not. When a bot posts something new, it's marked unread so you'll see it next time.

## What do bots remember?

Within a chat, a bot keeps the thread however long it gets: it summarises the older part in the background and reads the recent part word for word. Bots made from a template keep what they learn about your job in a notes document in the chat's folder, which anyone who can open the chat can open and correct. What a bot should always do goes in its additions, on its card in **Team**. See [Bots](/help/bots).

## How do bots use websites?

Each bot browses in its own browser, on its own computer. It can read pages, fill in forms and click through sites. When it needs you, for example to sign in, it hands you the screen with a **Your turn** card. Its browser in that chat stays signed in for next time, for everyone who can open the chat.

## Can bots work together?

Yes. A chat can have several bots in it, and people too: each bot answers in turn, or only the ones you mention with @. A bot can also ask another bot for something and wait for its answer, or pass a task on; the answer comes back to the chat you asked in, with a card. See [Messaging and working together](/help/messaging).

## Will a bot do anything without asking me?

Bots do safe steps on their own, such as reading and looking things up. They ask first before paying, ordering, booking, sending, cancelling, posting or inviting, with **Go ahead** and **Don't do it** in the chat. In **Settings** → **Permissions** you can add your own rules for what to always ask about and what to allow without asking. See [Approvals, safety and privacy](/help/approvals-and-privacy).

## Which AI model do my bots use?

Mercbot's own. You don't need to choose a model or enter any keys. There are two kinds, **Premium AI** and **Economy AI**, plus one for pictures; **Settings** → **Spending** shows what each costs.

## Who can see my chats?

Everyone in the home can open a **Public** chat, and every new chat is Public unless you choose **Private** when you start it. Only the members of a Private chat can open it, and nothing in it reaches other chats. A bot in a chat uses only what everyone who can open that chat can also open. See [Chats and your team](/help/chats).

## Do bots see my passwords?

No. Logins saved in the vault, and anything you type into a secure sign-in form, are filled in on the website by Mercbot itself and never reach the bot. When you sign in on the bot's computer yourself, your password goes straight to the website. Never paste passwords or card numbers into the chat. See [The vault](/help/vault).

## How much does Mercbot cost?

Mercbot has no plans or subscriptions. What your bots' work costs is what the AI providers bill for it: their AI calls and pictures. Their computers' days and their commands are counted too, at no cost. **Settings** → **Spending** shows what it came to.

## What does Spending show?

Open **Settings** → **Spending**, or choose **See spending** from the **Spending** row of the account menu. It shows this month's spending and a report of any day, week, month or range of days (in UTC): a chart by day, and the totals by kind, by bot and, for owners and admins, by person, which **Download CSV** saves as a spreadsheet file. Day-by-day totals are kept 13 months; older months keep their totals. Owners and admins see the whole home; members see their own use. What each bot cost is in the totals by bot; the bots themselves show no price.

## How is spending worked out?

Each piece of work is counted once it's done. An AI call counts what the AI provider says it cost, or else what it used at that provider's list prices; a call that fails costs nothing, and one cut off before it finishes is counted from its size, as an estimate. A computer counts one day the first time it starts each day (UTC), and a command the time it runs; neither costs anything. Spending is only reported: nothing is ever paused or refused because of it.

## Do I need my own AI account or key?

No. Your bots run on Mercbot's own AI service, so there's nothing to set up.

## Can I have more than one home?

Yes. Switch between your homes with the switcher at the top of the sidebar, where you can also create a new one. Each home has its own people, chats, bots, routines, apps and files.

## What are Apps?

Small apps your bots build on your home's own data, such as a customer list, a stock tracker or a public contact form. One bot, the Builder, makes and changes them, and you publish each one when you're happy. See [Apps](/help/apps).

## Is there a desktop or phone app?

Mercbot runs in your web browser, on a computer or a phone, so there's nothing to install. On a phone it uses a layout made for small screens, and you can add it to your home screen.

## Can I change the language?

Yes. Choose a language in **Settings** → **General**, and bots reply in it. The language and time zone are kept on each device.

## Can I change how Mercbot looks?

In **Settings** → **General**, choose a theme (follow your system, light or dark) and an accent colour. Each bot also has its own name, shape and colour.

## Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Press Ctrl+/ for the full list. Ctrl+K opens search, which finds chats, people, bots, routines, apps, settings, files and messages.

## How do I change my name or picture?

Open **Settings** → **General** and choose **Account details**. There you can change your display name and profile picture. Your email address can't be changed there.

## Can a bot have its own email address, or work in Slack, WhatsApp or Telegram?

Not yet. Bots can read and draft email in mailboxes you connect, and you talk to them in Mercbot itself.

## What's the difference between a skill and a routine?

A skill is *how* to do a task: saved steps a bot follows when asked. A routine is *when*: a job a bot does on a schedule, such as every Monday at 9. A routine can use a skill. See [Skills, routines and watches](/help/skills-and-routines).

## How do I make a skill or a routine?

For a skill, ask the bot to save what it just did as a skill, or teach it by recording yourself doing the task on its computer: in a chat with one bot, select **+**, then **Teach** and the bot's name, such as **Teach Ledger a task**. Type "/" in the message box of a chat with one bot to pick a skill: Mercbot's anywhere, and your own only in a Private chat where you're the only person. For a routine, just ask in the chat it should run in: "Every weekday at 8, check my calendar and list my meetings." The chat's **Chat details** lists its routines under **Routines**, where you can pause, edit or delete them; only the person who set a routine up can turn it back on.

## Can I share a bot with someone?

Inside a home, every bot is already everyone's: it's a member of the home, on the **Team** page, and anyone can start a chat with it or add it to one. See [Chats and your team](/help/chats).

Outside your home, you can share one of the ready-made bots in the [template gallery](/templates). A template's **Add to Mercbot** opens its share page: send someone that link, and its **Add to Mercbot** shows them everything the bot would be given before they add it to their own home. The gallery is curated by the Mercbot team, who alone add templates to it, so a bot you've made yourself can't go there, and Mercbot has no way to send your own bot to someone outside your home.

## Can I take a bot off my team?

Yes: archive it. Open its card in **Team** and, on its **Details** tab, select **Archive** and its name, such as **Archive Ledger**. It leaves the team for everyone in the home and stays in its chats without answering, and its routines and watches pause. Nothing is deleted, and its maker or the home's owners and admins can resume it. Only they can archive it, and the home's main bot and the bot that builds the home's apps can't be archived. See [Archive a bot](/help/bots#archive-a-bot).

## How do I delete a chat?

Right-click the chat in the sidebar, or open its **Chat details**, and choose **Delete** (in Chat details, **Delete chat**). It goes for everyone in it, with its whole history, its files and its bots' computers; its bots stay on your team. This can't be undone. The chat's maker and the home's owners and admins can delete it. To keep a Public chat readable for everyone instead, choose **Archive**. To take any chat out of your own list, a Private chat included, choose **Hide from sidebar**.

## How do I delete my account?

There's no button for this in Mercbot yet. Ask whoever runs your copy of Mercbot to delete it.
