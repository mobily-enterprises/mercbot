# General questions and answers

Short answers to the questions people ask most about Mercbot, with links to the pages that go into more detail.

## What is Mercbot?

Mercbot gives you bots that do real work for you. You ask in plain words, and a bot looks things up, works in websites, uses the apps you connect, makes documents and spreadsheets, and keeps going while you're away. Results arrive in your chat. See [Welcome to Mercbot](/help/welcome).

## How is a bot different from a chat assistant?

A bot doesn't just answer. It has its own computer in the cloud with a web browser and office apps, it can use your connected email, calendar and files, and it can do things on a schedule. It remembers lasting facts about you, asks before anything risky, and can hand work to your other bots.

## Who is Mercbot for?

Families, small businesses and companies. Everyone gets their own home, and can create more homes, such as one for the family and one for work. See [Example jobs for a bot](/help/example-jobs) for ideas.

## Where do my bots run?

On Mercbot's servers, not on your device. Each bot works on a small cloud computer: a desktop with a browser and office apps. You can watch it from the **Computer** tab in the bot's **Details** panel. See [The bots' computer and connected apps](/help/computer-and-apps).

## Do bots keep working when I close Mercbot?

Yes. A job you've started carries on in the background, and routines run at their set time whether you're online or not. When a bot posts something new, it's marked unread so you'll see it next time.

## What do bots remember?

Within a chat, a bot keeps the thread however long it gets: it summarises the older part in the background and reads the recent part word for word. Across chats, it remembers lasting facts you tell it, such as preferences or an allergy. All your bots share those facts, but your chats with each bot stay private.

## Can I see or correct what a bot remembers?

Yes. Open **Settings** → **Memory** to see what your bots know about you. Removing a fact there makes every bot forget it. To correct one, remove it and tell a bot the right version. A bot's memory is helpful, not a record to rely on, so keep important information in your own files or apps.

## How do bots use websites?

Each bot browses in its own browser, on its own computer. It can read pages, fill in forms and click through sites. When it needs you, for example to sign in, it hands you the screen with a **Your turn** card. Its browser keeps you signed in for next time.

## Can bots work together?

Yes. You can start a group chat with up to six bots, where each answers in turn, or only the ones you mention with @. A bot can also ask another bot for something and wait for its answer, or pass a task on for the other bot to answer you itself; both show as cards in the two chats. See [Messaging and working together](/help/messaging).

## Will a bot do anything without asking me?

Bots do safe steps on their own, such as reading and looking things up. They ask first before paying, ordering, booking, sending, cancelling, posting or inviting, with **Go ahead** and **Don't do it** in the chat. In **Settings** → **Permissions** you can add your own rules for what to always ask about and what to allow without asking. See [Approvals, safety and privacy](/help/approvals-and-privacy).

## Which AI model do my bots use?

Mercbot's own. You don't need to choose a model or enter any keys. There are two kinds, **Premium AI** and **Economy AI**, plus one for pictures; **Settings** → **Spending** shows what each costs.

## Who can see my chats?

Your chats with a bot are your own, even when other people in your home use the same bot. The exception is a **Shared** bot, whose conversations are open to everyone in the home, as the bot tells you. A **Mixed** bot may note things that help everyone in a home notebook, never anything personal, and tells you in a line you can undo. See [Bots shared in a home](/help/shared-bots).

## Do bots see my passwords?

No. Logins saved in your vault, and anything you type into a secure sign-in form, are filled in on the website by Mercbot itself and never reach the bot. When you sign in on the bot's computer yourself, your password goes straight to the website. Never paste passwords or card numbers into the chat. See [The vault](/help/vault).

## How much does Mercbot cost?

Mercbot has no plans or subscriptions. What your bots' work costs is what the AI providers bill for it: their AI calls and pictures. Their computers' days and their commands are counted too, at no cost. **Settings** → **Spending** shows what it came to.

## What does Spending show?

Open **Settings** → **Spending**, or choose **See spending** from the **Spending** row of the account menu. It shows this month's spending and a report of any day, week, month or range of days (in UTC): a chart by day, and the totals by kind, by bot and, for owners and admins, by person, which **Download CSV** saves as a spreadsheet file. Day-by-day totals are kept 13 months; older months keep their totals. Owners and admins see the whole home; members see their own use. Each bot also shows this month's figure beside its name in the sidebar.

## How is spending worked out?

Each piece of work is counted once it's done. An AI call counts what the AI provider says it cost, or else what it used at that provider's list prices; a call that fails costs nothing, and one cut off before it finishes is counted from its size, as an estimate. A computer counts one day the first time it starts each day (UTC), and a command the time it runs; neither costs anything. Spending is only reported: nothing is ever paused or refused because of it.

## Do I need my own AI account or key?

No. Your bots run on Mercbot's own AI service, so there's nothing to set up.

## Can I have more than one home?

Yes. Switch between your homes with the switcher at the top of the sidebar, where you can also create a new one. Each home has its own bots, routines, apps and files.

## What are Apps?

Small apps your bots build on your home's own data, such as a customer list, a stock tracker or a public contact form. One bot, the Builder, makes and changes them, and you publish each one when you're happy. See [Apps](/help/apps).

## Is there a desktop or phone app?

Mercbot runs in your web browser, on a computer or a phone, so there's nothing to install. On a phone it uses a layout made for small screens, and you can add it to your home screen.

## Can I change the language?

Yes. Choose a language in **Settings** → **General**, and bots reply in it. The language and time zone are kept on each device.

## Can I change how Mercbot looks?

In **Settings** → **General**, choose a theme (follow your system, light or dark) and an accent colour. Each bot also has its own name, shape and colour.

## Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Press Ctrl+/ for the full list. Ctrl+K opens search, which finds bots, routines, apps, settings, files and messages.

## How do I change my name or picture?

Open **Settings** → **General** and choose **Account details**. There you can change your display name and profile picture. Your email address can't be changed there.

## Can a bot have its own email address, or work in Slack, WhatsApp or Telegram?

Not yet. Bots can read and draft email in mailboxes you connect, and you talk to them in Mercbot itself.

## What's the difference between a skill and a routine?

A skill is *how* to do a task: saved steps a bot follows when asked. A routine is *when*: a job a bot does on a schedule, such as every Monday at 9. A routine can use a skill. See [Skills, routines and watches](/help/skills-and-routines).

## How do I make a skill or a routine?

For a skill, ask the bot to save what it just did as a skill, or teach it by recording yourself doing the task on its computer (**+** → **Teach a task**). Type "/" in the message box to pick a skill. For a routine, just ask: "Every weekday at 8, check my calendar and list my meetings." You can pause, edit or delete routines in the bot's Details panel.

## Can I share a bot with someone?

Inside a home, everyone already uses the same bots, and owners and admins choose whether each bot is **Private**, **Mixed** or **Shared**.

## How do I delete a bot?

Right-click the bot in the sidebar, choose **Delete** and confirm. Your first bot is your main bot and can't be deleted. If you'd only like it out of the way, choose **Hide from sidebar** instead.

## How do I delete my account?

There's no button for this in Mercbot yet. Ask whoever runs your copy of Mercbot to delete it.
