# Chats and your team

Everything you do in Mercbot happens in chats. A home's people and its bots are its members, side by side on the **Team** page, and a chat can have any of them in it: you and one bot, several bots, several people, or people only. This page explains the Team page, starting a chat, Public and Private chats, who answers, what belongs to a chat, the bots' computers in a chat, members, organising your chat list, and archiving.

## Your team

Select **Team** in the sidebar, beside **Files** and **Apps**. It lists everyone in the home: people with their role, and bots with who made them and their job.

- **Message** opens your chat with that bot, or starts a Private chat with that person.
- Select a person or a bot to open their card. A bot's card holds its instructions, Knowledge, files, computer, routines and Library.
- **Add bots** adds bots to the home. Bots you add appear here, and Mercbot's chat introduces them, so you can start a chat with each when you're ready.
- Bots that have left the home are listed under **Archived**, with **Resume**.

Your chat list starts empty. A chat appears when you start one, or when a bot writes to you.

## Start a chat

1. Select **New chat** (or press **Ctrl+Alt+G**).
2. In **To:**, add the people and bots you want. Start typing a name to find one.
3. Leave it **Public**, or choose **Private** (see below).
4. Give it a title if you like, write your first message and send it. Or press **Enter** with nothing typed to start it empty.

New chat also lists the home's Public chats you aren't in, so you can join one.

## Public and Private chats

Every new chat is **Public** unless you choose **Private** when you start it. You can't change it later.

| | Public | Private |
|---|---|---|
| Who can open it | Everyone in the home, including people who join later | Only its members |
| What its bots can use | Every Public chat in the home | Every Public chat, this chat, and other Private chats that all of this chat's members are in |
| Does it reach other chats? | Yes: bots can use it in any chat of the home | No: nothing in it reaches a chat someone else can open |

The rule behind this is simple: **a bot in a chat uses only what everyone who can open that chat can also open.** It doesn't matter who has actually looked.

The chat's header shows a globe for Public and a lock for Private, and the message box says who can read it, for example "Everyone in Rossi family can read this" or "Only you and Chef can read this".

Your chat with a bot is Public too, so everyone in the home can open it. For something only you should see, start a Private chat with just you and the bot.

## Who answers

- **When you're the only person in the chat,** every bot in it answers, in turn. Type **@** and pick a bot to ask only that one.
- **When several people are in the chat,** a bot answers only when it's called: by its name, with **@**, when you reply to one of its messages, or with **@everyone**.
- After the bots answer, a bot another bot mentioned may answer once more. Bots stay quiet when they have nothing new to add.
- A chat with people only has no bot answering at all.

Approval cards are always for the person whose message started the work. Only they can answer them.

## What belongs to a chat

What a bot makes in a chat belongs to that chat: its files, pages, routines and watches, and the notes it keeps for its job. Anyone who can open the chat can open them, and nobody else can.

A bot never puts something where more people can see it by itself. To keep a file in the bot's own folder or the home's shared folder from a Private chat, it asks you first with a card.

## The bots' computers

In a chat, each bot has its own computer, labelled with its name, such as "Chef's computer". With several bots, each one's computer is listed separately.

- Everyone who can open the chat can watch a bot's computer and use what's signed in on it. The computer says so, and shows what's signed in there.
- When a bot signs in to a site there, it says so in one line: "Everyone who can open this chat can use this sign-in."
- Anyone who can open the chat can **Take over** a bot's computer. While you have control, the bot leaves it alone; hand it back when you're done. The chat shows a line when someone takes over and when they hand back.

## Members

- Anyone in a chat can add people and bots from the home. Adding a person to a Private chat warns you that they will see everything said there.
- The person who started the chat, and the home's owners and admins, can remove members, rename the chat, archive it or delete it.
- Anyone can leave a chat. A Private chat is deleted when its last person leaves.
- Your chat with a bot takes no other members. To bring someone in, start a new chat.
- The home's chat for everyone, when it has one, has everyone in the home in it and stays pinned at the top for each person. Nobody can leave it.

## Organise your chat list

The sidebar lists your chats: pinned ones at the top, then the rest (in your own sections if you have any), then **Hidden chats**. Rows are sorted by latest activity, and a dot marks a chat with something you haven't read. An unsent draft stays with each chat on your device.

Right-click a chat to pin or unpin it, move it to one of your sections, mark it as read or unread, or hide it. Pins, sections, hidden chats and unread marks are yours alone: other people organise their own lists.

To jump between chats, press **Ctrl+Alt+1** to **9** for the chats in sidebar order, **Alt+↑** and **Alt+↓** for the previous or next one, or **Ctrl+K** to search.

## Archive and delete

- **Archive a Public chat** to put it away for everyone. The person who started it, and owners and admins, can.
- **Archive a Private chat** to put it away for yourself.
- **Delete a chat** to remove it for good, with its files and its bots' computers.

When a bot is archived, it has left the home: it stays in its chats, greyed out, and never answers. Its routines and watches pause. Its chat with you says it has left, and whoever made it, or an owner or admin, can resume it from **Team**.

## Questions

### Can other people read my chats?

Everyone in the home can open a Public chat, your chat with a bot included. Only the members of a Private chat can open it.

### Does a bot tell other chats what I said in a Private chat?

No. Nothing in a Private chat reaches a chat that someone else can open.

### Can I make a Public chat Private later?

No. A chat stays the kind it started as. Start a new Private chat instead.
