# Views: live figures from your home's data

A view shows figures from your home's tables and stays up to date by itself. Ask any bot for "open deals by stage as a chart, and the ten biggest", and it makes one straight away, with nothing to publish. It sits between a page, which is written once, and an app, which people work in.

## What a view is

A view is one screen of lists, totals and charts on your home's tables. It holds the question, not a copy of the answer, so it's always current: when someone adds or changes a record, an open view shows it within a few seconds.

A view only shows things. It has no forms or buttons, so nothing you do in it can change your data.

## Ask for a view

Ask any bot in your own words, for example:

- "Show me this month's invoices by status, and keep it up to date."
- "Make a chart of contacts by stage."
- "List the ten biggest open deals."

The figures have to be in your home's tables. If they aren't, the bot says so, and your Builder can make a table for them.

The bot answers with a card in the chat, showing the top of the view. Click it to open the whole view.

## Change a view

Ask the bot again: "make it a pie and add the total". It changes the same view rather than making another, and keeps the earlier version.

To go back, open the view and choose **Versions**, then **Restore** on the one you want. Restoring makes it the newest version, so nothing is lost.

## Who can see a view

Everyone in your home can see every view from the moment it's saved, whichever chat it was asked for in, and every bot in the home can find it. Nothing inside a home is private, so there's nothing to share: an app can use a new view straight away.

A view only shows figures that everyone in the home can already see in the Data tab.

## Find a view again

- the bot's **Library**, in its Details panel, where you can also pin it
- the **Apps** area, marked **View**, with a filter for apps or views
- search, by its title
- the sidebar, once you pin it from the Apps area; views and apps share the same 8 pins

## Views inside apps

Your Builder can put a view on an app's screen. It can do this in two ways, and you can ask for either:

- **Shared:** the app always shows the view as it is now. One change reaches every app that shares it, so only the Builder can change such a view, and it can't be retired while an app uses it.
- **A copy:** the app keeps its own copy, which stays the same until you decide. When the original changes, the app asks whether to update. **Update** puts the new version in the app's draft, for you to preview and publish. **Not now** leaves the app as it is until the next change.

Views can't appear on an app's public screens yet.

## Retire a view

Nothing is ever deleted. When you no longer want a view, open it and choose **Retire**. Anyone in the home can retire a view. It's refused while an app shares the view, and the message says which app.

A retired view leaves the Apps area, search and your pins, and apps can't use it any more. It and its versions are kept, your data stays in your tables, and an app's copy of the view stays too.

Anyone in the home can bring one back: open **Apps**, choose **Retired**, then **Bring back** on the view. It comes back as it was. If another app or view has its name by then, it comes back with a number after its name, such as "Deals (2)", so the two can't be mixed up.

## View, page or app?

- A **view** for figures that should stay current.
- A **page** for writing to keep: a guide, a plan, a checklist.
- An **app** when people need to enter or change things. See [Apps: what they are and when to use them](apps.md).

## Questions

**Why does my view say it can't be changed?** An app shares it. Ask your Builder to change it, or ask your bot for a new view.

**Can I make a view public?** Not yet. Views are only for people in your home.

**Can a view show data from a connected app?** Only once that data is in one of your home's tables.
