# Welcome to Mercbot

Mercbot gives you bots that take on real work for you. You tell a bot what you need in plain words, and it gets on with it: it looks things up, works in websites, reads and drafts email, checks your calendar, makes documents and spreadsheets, and keeps going on its own computer while you do something else. When the work is done, the result arrives in your chat. This page explains what Mercbot is, what makes it different, and how to hand over a first job that goes well.

## What Mercbot does

Each bot is a helper with its own name, face and job. You talk to it in a chat, the same way you'd message a colleague. Behind the chat, the bot can:

- browse the web in its own browser, on its own small computer;
- use the apps you connect in **Connect apps**, such as your email, calendar, files or GitHub;
- make real files: documents, spreadsheets, slide decks, PDFs, charts and pictures;
- remember lasting facts you tell it, such as an allergy or how you like things done;
- do things on a schedule, through routines you set up by asking;
- pass a task to another of your bots.

You can have several bots, each with a clear job. One might plan the family's meals, another might keep an eye on supplier contracts, a third might prepare for sales calls.

## What sets Mercbot apart

### Bots with their own computer

Every bot works on a computer of its own in the cloud: a small desktop with a web browser and office apps. Open the bot's **Details** panel and choose the **Computer** tab to watch it work. When a step needs you, such as signing in to a website, the bot hands you the screen. You do that step yourself, then hand it back. Your password goes straight to the website and the bot never sees it.

Because the computer lives in the cloud, the bot keeps going when you close the tab. A long errand carries on in the background, and a routine runs at its time whether you're there or not.

### Homes

Everything in Mercbot lives in a **home**: its bots, their files and computers, and the people who use them. You get your own home to start with, and can create more homes for different parts of your life, such as one for the family and one for a small business. Each person's chats with a bot stay their own, and a home's owners decide how much each bot shares. See [Homes](/help/homes).

### Apps

Bots can also build small apps on your home's own data: a customer list, a stock tracker, leave requests, or a public contact form. One bot in each home is the **Builder**. You describe what you want, it prepares a draft, and you publish it when you're happy. Every bot can then read and add to that data from chat. Nothing is ever truly deleted, and every change is recorded. See [Apps](/help/apps).

### You stay in control

Bots ask before anything that sends, buys, books, posts or deletes. An approval appears in the chat with **Go ahead** and **Don't do it**. You can fine-tune this with your own rules in Settings. See [Approvals, safety and privacy](/help/approvals-and-privacy).

## Handing over a good first job

Bots do their best work when they know what "done" looks like. A good first job is:

- **Specific.** "Find three Italian restaurants near the station with a table for four on Friday at 8" works better than "find somewhere to eat".
- **Checkable.** Ask for the result in a form you can look over: a short list, a table, a draft email or a spreadsheet.
- **Clear about limits.** Say what the bot mustn't do, such as "don't book anything yet" or "only use my work calendar".
- **Something you'd otherwise spend twenty minutes on.** Research, comparing options, tidying a list, drafting a reply, or summarising a long document are good places to start.

If you're not sure where to start, your first bot will suggest a few ideas, and [Example jobs for a bot](/help/example-jobs) has more.

When the bot replies, read what it did. If something isn't right, say so in the chat, just as you would with a person. It keeps the whole conversation in mind.

## Questions

### Does my bot keep working when I'm offline?

Yes. A bot's work runs on Mercbot's servers, not in your browser. You can close the tab or switch off your computer. Routines run at their set time, and the bot posts the result in its chat and marks itself unread, so you'll see it next time you look.

### Does it remember things?

Yes, in two ways. Within a chat, the bot keeps track of the whole conversation, however long it gets. Across chats, it remembers lasting facts you tell it, such as preferences or allergies. All your bots share these facts, and you can see and remove them in **Settings** → **Memory**.

### Can it use websites that need a login?

Yes. The bot can use a login you've saved in your vault, ask you to fill in a secure form, or hand you the screen to sign in yourself. Its browser keeps you signed in for next time. See [The vault](/help/vault).

### What does it cost?

There are no plans. What your bots' work costs is what the AI providers bill for it; **Settings** → **Spending** shows what each bot cost this month. See [General questions and answers](/help/faq).
