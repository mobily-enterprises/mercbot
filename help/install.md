# Install Mercbot as an app

Mercbot installs straight from its own address, on a phone, a tablet or a computer: there's no app store and nothing separate to download. Installed, it opens from its own icon, full screen, without the browser's address bar. It can show notifications when a bot has news, and other apps can share photos, files and links into it. It's the same Mercbot, with the same account, homes, bots and chats, so everything you do there is everywhere you use Mercbot.

## On Android

1. Open Mercbot's address in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Open the account menu and choose **Install app**. Chrome shows its own install sheet; tap **Install**.
3. Open Mercbot from its icon on your home screen.

On a phone, Mercbot also offers **Install** once, at the top of the screen, a few seconds after you start. **Not now** puts it away for good; **Install app** stays in the account menu.

If neither shows, open Chrome's menu and choose **Add to home screen** or **Install app**.

## On an iPhone or iPad

Safari installs Mercbot from its Share menu:

1. Open Mercbot's address in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Mercbot from its icon on your Home Screen.

**Install app** in the account menu shows these steps too. Notifications on an iPhone or iPad work only once Mercbot is on your Home Screen, opened from its icon.

## On a computer

In Chrome or Edge, open the account menu and choose **Install app**, or use the install button at the end of the address bar. Mercbot then opens in its own window, and from the Start menu, the Dock or the app launcher.

## What changes once it's installed

- **Its own window:** full screen on a phone, with the phone's status bar in Mercbot's colours, light or dark as you chose in Settings.
- **Quick actions:** press and hold the icon (on Android) or right-click it (on a computer) for **New chat**, **Recurring tasks** and **Files**.
- **The icon's count:** on Android and computers, the icon shows how many of your chats have something new, in all your homes. Chats you muted aren't counted.
- **Opening links:** a link to another website opens in your browser, not inside Mercbot.
- **Offline:** without a connection, Mercbot shows a page that says so, with **Try again**; it carries on as soon as you're back online. Bots keep working on Mercbot's servers meanwhile.

## Notifications

Notifications tell you when a routine or a watch has results, when a bot is waiting for you (a question, an approval, a draft to check or an account to connect), and about messages and mentions. Each one opens its chat. A notification waits about 15 seconds and goes only if you haven't read the chat in Mercbot by then, and a chat's notifications show as one.

Turn them on in **Settings → General → Notifications**, on each device you want them on. Mercbot also offers them once, just after you set up your first routine. See [Settings and notifications](/help/settings#notifications) for the switches, quiet hours and devices.

## Sharing into Mercbot

On Android and on a computer, once Mercbot is installed, it's in the share menu of other apps:

1. In another app, choose **Share**, then **Mercbot**.
2. Mercbot opens with **Send to a chat**. Choose the chat, or type part of its name to find it.
3. What you shared is in the message box: its words and links in the text, its photos and files attached. Add anything you like, then send it.

Sharing into Mercbot isn't available on an iPhone or iPad.

## Questions

### Do I need to install Mercbot to use it on my phone?

No. Mercbot works in your phone's browser too. Installing gives it its own icon and window, and on an iPhone or iPad it's what makes notifications possible.

### How do I uninstall it?

As you'd remove any app: press and hold its icon and choose to remove it, or on a computer use **Uninstall** in its window's menu. Your account, bots and chats stay as they are.

### I installed Mercbot. Why don't I get notifications?

Notifications are turned on per device: open **Settings → General → Notifications** on that device. If it says they're blocked, allow them for Mercbot in the browser's or the phone's settings. A muted chat never notifies, and nothing comes during your quiet hours.

### Does the installed app update itself?

Yes. It's always the version on Mercbot's address, so a new release reaches it the next time it opens.
