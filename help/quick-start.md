# Quick start: your first finished job

This page takes you from your first visit to a finished job you can check and repeat. Mercbot runs in your web browser. Set aside about fifteen minutes.

## Before you start

You need:

- an up-to-date web browser on a computer (a phone works too, but a bigger screen is easier the first time);
- a small, real job in mind, such as researching something, comparing a few options or drafting a reply.

If you want the bot to use your email, calendar or files, have those accounts' sign-ins to hand.

## 1. Open Mercbot

On the computer that runs your copy, `mercbot open` opens Mercbot in your browser (see [Running your own copy of Mercbot](/help/own-copy)). A copy that asks you to sign in explains how in its own help.

## 2. Meet your first bot

The first time you open Mercbot, three short slides introduce it. Then your first bot appears. It's called Mercbot and starts out black.

1. The bot greets you by your first name.
2. It asks you to pick a name for it. Choose one of its suggestions. The new name shows everywhere at once, and you can rename it later.
3. It explains how it works and offers to connect your email. You can do that now with the card in the chat, or skip it and just tell the bot what you need.

This first bot is your main bot. You can create more later with **New bot** in the sidebar.

## 3. Give it a first job

Type your request in the message box and press Enter. Be specific and say what you want back. For example:

> Find three family-friendly campsites within two hours of Leeds that are open in April. Put them in a table with price per night, distance and one line on why each is good. Don't book anything.

While the bot works, a status line describes what it's doing, and its face changes expression. When it has finished, the whole reply arrives at once.

If the bot needs a decision from you, it asks a short question, sometimes with answers you can tap.

## 4. Connect the apps it needs

To work with your email, calendar, files or code, a bot needs you to connect those accounts.

1. Select **Connect apps** in the sidebar.
2. Find the app, for example Gmail or Google Calendar, using the categories or search.
3. Select **Connect**, then **Sign in** beside the account. A new tab opens on the app's own sign-in and permission page.
4. Sign in and allow access. Back in Connect apps, the account shows as **Connected** after a few seconds.

You can switch off single actions for an app, such as sending email, in that app's **Tools** list. Bots always ask before sending, forwarding or deleting anything. See [The bots' computer and connected apps](/help/computer-and-apps).

## 5. Let it sign in to websites

Some jobs need a website where you have an account. When the bot reaches a sign-in, it tries these in order:

1. a login you gave it for this task in your message;
2. a login saved in your vault for that site;
3. a secure form in the chat, where you type the username and password. The bot never sees them, and you can tick **Save securely to your vault** for next time;
4. handing you the screen.

When the bot hands you the screen, a **Your turn** card appears. Select **Take over** to open the bot's computer, sign in yourself, then select **I'm done**. If you'd rather not, select **Skip**. The bot then checks the page and carries on.

To save logins ahead of time, open **Settings** → **Vault** and add a **New item**. See [The vault](/help/vault).

## 6. Check the result

When the reply arrives:

- **Read it through.** If the bot used websites, its reply ends with a screenshot of each page it used.
- **Open files.** Documents, spreadsheets, slides and PDFs arrive as file cards. Select one to open a viewer with a download.
- **Watch the computer.** Open the bot's Details panel (select its name above the chat) and choose the **Computer** tab.
- **Find it later.** The Details panel's **Library** tab lists everything the bot made, by day.

If something isn't right, reply in the chat: "The second campsite is closed in April, find another." The bot picks up from where it left off.

## 7. Make it repeat

Once a job works, you can have it done again without asking each time:

- **On a schedule:** ask in plain words, such as "Every Monday at 9, check these campsites for April availability." The bot sets up a routine and tells you when it will run, with your time zone.
- **As a skill:** ask the bot to save what it did as a skill, or teach it a task by recording yourself doing it on its computer.

See [Skills, routines and watches](/help/skills-and-routines).

## On your phone

Mercbot also works in your phone's browser, with a layout made for small screens, and you can add it to your home screen.

## If something goes wrong

- A bot that seems stuck, a sign-in that won't work or an app that needs signing in again: see [Fixing common problems](/help/troubleshooting).
- Anything else: ask whoever runs your copy of Mercbot.
