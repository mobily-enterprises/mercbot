# App templates

Templates are ready-made apps you can add to your home in a moment and then shape to fit. Each one comes with its tables, fields and screens already worked out, so you start from something that works rather than a blank page. This page lists the templates, explains how to install one, what it brings and what it leaves out, and how to adapt it with the Builder.

## Browse the templates

Open **Apps** and choose the **Templates** tab. Each template shows:

- its icon, colour and name
- a **Public** badge if it's made to be used by people outside your home
- a sentence on what it's for
- how many screens it has and which tables it uses

## The six templates

| Template | What it's for | Tables | Screens you'll see |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CRM** | Contacts and the deals you're working on | Contacts, Deals | Contacts, Deals, and a Pipeline with a bar chart of deals by stage and the value at each stage |
| **Stock** | Items, how many you have and where, and when to reorder | Items | Items, and By location with a pie chart of quantity by location |
| **Leave requests** | People ask for time off; someone approves or declines it | Leave requests | Requests, Ask for leave, and a Summary with a chart by status |
| **Hiring pipeline** | Candidates for each role and how far they've got | Candidates | Candidates, and a Pipeline with a chart by stage |
| **Contact form** | A public form anyone can send, and a list of what came in | Enquiries | Enquiries inside your home; a public form and thank-you screen for visitors |
| **Bookings** | People sign in with their email, book, and see and change only their own bookings | Bookings | All bookings inside your home; My bookings and Book for visitors |

Contact form and Bookings have public screens for visitors, with their visitor access. They work inside your home straight away.

## Install a template

A home's owners and admins install templates from the Templates tab:

1. Find the template you want.
2. Press **Install**. Where a template offers it, press **With sample data** instead to add a couple of example records, so you can see how it looks in use.
3. The new app opens as a draft, with a note such as "CRM is ready as a draft. Preview it, then publish."
4. Look around the draft. A draft preview works on practice data, a copy of your records: you can add and change records there freely, and none of it reaches your real records. **Reset** starts the copy again. With **With sample data**, the sample records are added to your real tables when you install, so the preview shows them too.
5. Press **Publish** when you're happy, or ask the Builder for changes first.

Everyone else in the home can ask the Builder instead: "Install the Leave requests template." The Builder installs it in the same way and posts the usual draft card with **Preview** and **Publish**.

## What a template includes

- **Tables and fields**, with sensible types and choices, such as a deal's stage (New, Proposal, Won, Lost) or a booking's status (Requested, Confirmed, Cancelled)
- **Screens and navigation**: lists, forms for adding and editing, single records, and charts or totals where they help
- **Visitor access** for the public templates, such as letting visitors add enquiries but read nothing
- **Sample records**, only if you choose **With sample data**, and only for the templates that have them (CRM, Stock, Leave requests and Hiring pipeline)

## What a template leaves out

- **Your data.** Apart from optional samples, a template adds no records. Your existing records are never touched.
- **Watches and scripts.** If you want a bot to react to new records, or logic beyond what the screens do, ask for it after installing.
- **Settings.** The app's look stays as you set it.

## Templates and the tables you already have

Templates fit around your home's existing data:

- If your home already has a table with the same name, the template **reuses it**, adding only the fields it's missing.
- Nothing is removed or renamed in your tables.
- If an existing field doesn't fit the template, for example it has a different type, the app is still added, but the note says its draft needs the Builder. Ask the Builder to sort it out before publishing.
- Sample records that don't fit your existing tables, for example because a choice is different, are left out, and the note says how many.

So you can, for example, install CRM after building your own contacts list, and both apps will share the same contacts.

## Adapt a template with the Builder

An installed template is an ordinary app. Change it the way you change any app:

- Press **Ask Builder** on any screen, or use **Point and ask** to pick a part, and describe what you want: "add a city to contacts", "show only open deals", "add a button that asks the Sales bot to research this company".
- Ask for a different look, such as your brand's colours and fonts.
- Ask for a watch, such as "tell me when a new enquiry arrives".

Each change goes into a draft for you to preview and publish. See [Build an app with the Builder](/help/apps-builder).

## Questions

### Can I install the same template twice?
Yes. You get a second app. It reuses the same tables, so both apps show the same records.

### Why can't I see Install?
Only a home's owners and admins install templates from this tab. Ask the Builder to install one for you.

### Can I remove the sample records?
Yes. Mark them obsolete in the **Data** tab, like any record.
